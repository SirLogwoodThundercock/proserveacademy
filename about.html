About Pro Serve Academy

Training and placement for professional household and personal service staff. We prepare skilled, polished and trustworthy people for careers in private homes.

Who we are: Training and placement excellence

Pro Serve Academy is a training academy and placement agency for professional household and personal service staff.

Our mission

To train, certify and place exceptional service professionals who deliver reliability, discretion and excellence in every home and household they serve.

Our vision

To be the first name families, estates and employers think of when they need trusted domestic and personal service professionals, and the first choice for anyone building a career in the field.

Our values

Excellence, Integrity, Discretion, Respect, and Growth define who we are and guide everything we do.

The Academy and Agency working together

Pro Serve Academy operates in two connected parts: training professionals and placing them with households that need them.

01

The Academy

Practical, standards-driven training for household and personal service roles, covering technical skills and professional conduct.

02

The Agency

Matching trained professionals with households by understanding needs, routines, and expectations for perfect fit.

03

House Managers

Coordinating staff, schedules, vendors, maintenance, inventory and household budgets with organisation and discretion.

04

Housekeepers & Chefs

Professional cleaning standards, fabric care, laundry, organisation, menu planning, food preparation and kitchen hygiene.

05

Drivers & Caretakers

Safe, punctual chauffeurs with defensive driving, and compassionate caretakers for children, elderly and those needing care.
